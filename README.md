# Shubham & Manasi: शुभ विवाह invitation

Marathi wedding invitation for Shubham & Manasi, 2–3 November 2026, Chandani Lawn, Pune.

Tap सावधान to open: the mangalashtak plays and the antarpaat comes down. Then scroll through the pandal, the akshata and maap films, the invitation letter, the calendar (tap a glowing date), the day-by-day events, the venue, the diya (tap to light it) and the closing gate.

Personal links: add `?to=Joshi-Family` to greet a family by name.

Static site: one `index.html` plus `assets/`. Painted layers generated with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5). Music: "Sound of the Valleys" by Siddharth Parashar (Epidemic Sound). Sound effects are optional files in `assets/audio/` (tutari, bell, cloth); a built-in synth fills in when one is missing.
